# Credit_card_dashboard
Credit Card Customer & Transaction Report (Power BI + SQL)
Examples of data cleaning, SQL data modeling, and interactive dashboard visualization using Power BI, showing below Visualizations & Insights:

- Revenue by Card Category (Blue, Silver, Gold, Platinum) — Blue card dominates with $47M of $57M total revenue
- Quarterly Revenue and Total Transactions — Revenue stable ($13.8M–$14.5M) while Q4 transactions spike to 173.2K
- Revenue by Expenditure Type (Bills, Entertainment, Fuel, Grocery, Food, Travel) — Bills highest at $14M
- Revenue by Education Level (Graduate $22.8M, High School $11.4M, etc.)
- Revenue by Profession (Businessman $17.7M, White-collar $10.3M, etc.)
- Revenue by Age Group & Gender (40–50 age group highest at $24.7M)
- Revenue by Income Group (High $21M, Medium $10M, Low $10M)
- Revenue by Marital Status (Married $16M vs Single $13M)
- Revenue by Dependent Count, Top 5 States (TX, NY, CA, FL, NJ), and Payment Method (Swipe $36M)
- KPI Cards: Total Revenue $57M | Interest Earned $8M | Total Income $588M | CSS 3.19

Key Insights:
- Blue card category is the primary revenue driver (~82% of total revenue)
- Bills and Entertainment are the top expenditure categories
- Graduates and Businessmen contribute the most to revenue
- Swipe remains the dominant transaction method ($36M vs $17M for other methods combined)
- Revenue is geographically concentrated in TX, NY, CA, FL, and NJ
